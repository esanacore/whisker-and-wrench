Whisker & WrenchDemo: what we make, in two minutes

Free, picture-first furniture plans and practical software for animal shelters and animal control.

What's real here: this page runs on its own with no server and no network. The stepper below replays three of the eleven steps from the real Plan 001 guide with simplified drawings. The real guide draws every part, has a full shopping list, and lives at whiskerandwrench.com/plans/001-shelter-cot/. Plan 001 is a draft that hasn't been build-tested yet.

  • Furniture plansRaised dog cots, cat towers and more, built only from parts on the shelf at Home Depot or Lowe's. Each plan is a LEGO-style guide: one step at a time, new parts in yellow.
  • For sheltersPlans are free. Hands-on help is sliding scale, and a shelter can fund it with its own fundraiser. See For shelters.
  • Software (research)An admin app for records, calls and dispatch, and an adopter app for new pet parents. We're talking with shelters and animal control officers first.
