First weeks home

Walk through five questions for the first weeks after adopting a dog. Pebble is fictional. This checklist is not veterinary or legal advice.

Sample checklist

Topic 1 of 5. Food for the first days.

Food for the first days

Ask Sample Shelter which food Pebble has been eating, how much, and whether to keep that food the same for now. Changing foods is a decision for the shelter or a veterinarian.

Grooming questions

Ask which brush they use, when Pebble last had a bath, and whether nails should be trimmed by shelter staff or a groomer. Do not use products made for people.

Prevention questions

Ask what flea, tick, and heartworm prevention was last given, and when a veterinarian should decide the next one. This page does not name products or doses.

Vaccine papers

Collect the vaccine and microchip papers Sample Shelter provided. Ask which ones a veterinarian should review, and book the visit the shelter recommends.

Town registration

Ask Sample Shelter or the town clerk whether Pebble needs a license, what proof to bring, and the deadline. Rules differ by town.

Questions to confirm

  • Which food Pebble has been eating, and whether to keep it the same.
  • Which brush to use, and who should trim nails.
  • What prevention was already given, and when a veterinarian decides the next one.
  • Which vaccine and microchip papers to take to the vet visit.
  • Whether the town requires a license, and by when.

Confirm each one with Sample Shelter or a veterinarian. Whisker & Wrench does not sell pet insurance. If the shelter mentions insurance, read the exclusions before you buy a plan.
