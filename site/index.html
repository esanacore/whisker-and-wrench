Better beds. Better tools. Happier animals.

Whisker & Wrench makes free furniture plans that a volunteer can build with one trip to the hardware store, and simple software for the people who run shelters and answer animal control calls.

What we build

Furniture plans

Shelter gear from the plumbing aisle

Raised dog cots, kitty cots, cat towers and more. Every part is stocked at Home Depot or Lowe's. No specialty fittings, no online orders.

Each plan has a picture-first, step-by-step guide, like a LEGO manual.

PVC Shelter Cot

A raised, washable dog bed made from 1″ PVC pipe, standard tees and elbows, and a vinyl panel. About $40 to $55 in parts and 90 minutes of work.

Why this exists

Easy to clean

Shelters need gear they can hose down and disinfect. Carpet and wood soak up everything. PVC and vinyl don't.

Easy to buy

Most DIY plans need furniture-grade fittings you have to order online. Ours only use parts on the shelf at a big-box store.

Easy to follow

Guides are pictures first, words second. If you can follow a LEGO manual, you can build this.

Built with shelters

We're shaping every plan and app with the people who do this work every day: shelter staff, volunteers and animal control officers.
