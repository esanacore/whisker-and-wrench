# App vision

## Two apps

**Admin app (shelter staff, animal control officers)**
- Animal records, people records (adopters, owners, complainants, volunteers)
- Calls and complaints, dispatch, field visits, notes and photos
- Intake, medical, kennel, adoption, licensing

**Adopter app (new pet parents)**
- Care guides: grooming, diet, pest prevention (fleas, ticks, heartworm), training, first weeks home
- Vaccine records and reminders
- Pet registration / licensing help
- Pet insurance info (referrals; actually selling insurance needs a license)
- Adoption paperwork and shelter contact

## Open questions

- **One app per shelter vs one shared app.** Apple generally rejects near-identical apps built from a template unless each organization submits it under its own developer account. Options:
  - White-label, each shelter publishes under its own account (Apple and Google offer nonprofit fee waivers).
  - One shared adopter app where users pick their shelter. Cheaper to maintain, and a family keeps all their records in one place even across shelters.
  - Verify the current App Store Review Guidelines before deciding.
- Build on / integrate with ASM, Shelterluv, PetPoint, or build our own backend?
- Offline support for field officers (low signal areas).
- Data privacy for people records and complaint/case data.
- Revenue: per-shelter subscription on a sliding scale, insurance referrals, sponsorships.
