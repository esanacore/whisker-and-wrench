# Dispatch to intake facilitator guide

Set aside 20 minutes with a dispatcher or field officer and a shelter intake worker. Open the Software preview from the home page. Use the fictional case for both runs.

1. Walk through assignment, field readiness and shelter receipt. Pause between readiness and receipt to ask what evidence intake needs to confirm the transfer. Ask each participant to explain what they know at each step and what their role needs from the previous person.
2. Refresh and run the intake-unavailable path. Ask who has custody, who finds a receiving location, and how a change is communicated. Retry, mark the case ready, and select Cannot confirm receipt. Ask how the teams reconcile an unconfirmed receipt. Retry and confirm to close the handoff.
3. Compare the two runs with the team's actual process. For each event, record the responsible role, the minimum information it needs, who confirms it, and where the official record lives.
4. Ask where the preview breaks down. Discuss missing identification, no connection in the field, or a handoff that never receives confirmation. Record which exception matters most and how the team resolves it today.

For each finding, capture the step, the role affected, what is missing, and a fictional example. Keep case records, addresses and contact information out of shared notes and public issues.

Use the findings to choose the next workflow slice. Confirm which system owns the case reference and the intake record before specifying storage or an integration.
