# Shelter and animal control software landscape (Oct 2026)

Light first pass. Verify details with vendors before relying on them.

## Major shelter platforms

- **Shelterluv**, **PetPoint**: the big shelter management systems.
- **ShelterBuddy**: developed by RSPCA Queensland.
- **Chameleon (CMS)**: kennel management plus field staff dispatching, licensing, mapping, barcoding. The one most aimed at animal control.

## Open source

- **Animal Shelter Manager (ASM)**: open source with a free version (hosted plans start around $340/user/year per Capterra). Covers animal control (dispatching field personnel), incidents, case management, intake, foster, medical, volunteers, fundraising. Web, Android and iOS, but reviewers say the mobile app needs a redesign. Strong candidate to integrate with or build on.
  - https://www.capterra.com/p/179181/Animal-Shelter-Manager/

## Small / foster-focused tools

Pawlytics, Trackabeast (about $25/month), Buzz to the Rescues, BARRK, Shelter Pro, Shelter Boss (includes dog licensing and animal control), PawPlacer (free plan), Animlly, FurryHelpDesk.
- https://www.capterra.com.au/alternatives/179205/shelterluv

## Municipal code enforcement

- **Comcate**: mobile field inspections, photos, case notes, notices of violation. No dispatch described.
  - https://www.comcate.com/software-solutions

## Gaps we noticed

- No product leads with a polished **adopter-facing app**.
- Mobile/field experience is weak in at least the open-source option.
- Shelters are slow to switch records systems, so integrating beats replacing early on.
