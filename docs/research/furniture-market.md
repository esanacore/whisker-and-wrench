# Shelter furniture: what's out there (Oct 2026)

## Beds

- **Kuranda** is the standard elevated bed in shelters. Chew-proof warranty, easy to sanitize. Their **ShelterBeds** program lets a shelter post a wish list; donors buy discounted beds shipped straight to the shelter (free shipping in the lower 48).
  - https://kuranda.com/faq/what-is-the-kuranda-shelterbeds-donation-program/
- **HGTV DIY PVC cot**: 1" Sch 40, about $30, but needs furniture-grade 3-way corner fittings that big-box stores don't carry (must order online).
  - https://www.hgtv.com/decorating/diy/raised-dog-cot-bed
- **Instructables elevated dog bed**: 1.25" PVC, based on a rescue's video; tip to secure fabric corners against chewing.
  - https://www.instructables.com/Elevated-dog-bed
- **Formufit large dog bed plan**: free PDF/SketchUp, aimed at shelters, uses Formufit's own furniture-grade fittings.
  - https://formufit.com/products/pvc-large-dog-bed-plan

**Our angle:** standard plumbing-aisle tees and elbows only, plus picture-first guides.

## Cat furniture

- Shelters say carpeted cat trees are hard to disinfect and fall apart. They prefer wipeable plastic or metal, and Kuranda or Crijo towers.
  - https://forum.maddiesfund.org/discussion/free-roam-cat-room-design
  - https://petfinderfoundation.org/grant_report/mi557-7/
- Disinfectable towers and cots are tied to lower disease transmission and less stress (Petfinder Foundation grant report, SPCA Serving Erie County).
  - https://petfinderfoundation.org/?p=38739
- UT Dallas EPICS student project spec for a shelter kitten tree: disassemble in under 2 minutes, withstand shelter cleaners, support 30 lb, no wobble. Good requirements template for Plan 003.
  - https://sites.utdallas.edu/epics-richardson-animal-shelter/files/2018/10/Semester-Project-Plan-final.pdf
- Catio/disinfection: shelters want no wood, fully disinfectable materials.
  - https://forum.maddiesfund.org/discussion/building-a-catiooutdoor-cat-enclosure
