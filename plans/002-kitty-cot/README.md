# Plan 002: Kitty Cot

**Status: measuring sheet only. Not a build guide.**

A Kitty Cot is a small raised bed that fits inside a cat kennel. Kennels are not one size, and no test cot has been built. This folder has no pipe lengths, no shopping list, and no cut list.

`measure.html` is the sheet a volunteer prints and fills at a real kennel. It asks for interior width, depth, and height, the door opening, and anything on the floor the legs must clear. The numbers stay on that paper.

Do not turn this into a guide until someone has measured a real kennel and a test cot has been built and checked against those numbers.
