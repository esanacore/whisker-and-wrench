Kitty CotMeasure the kennel first

A Kitty Cot has to fit one real cat kennel. Kennels differ, and no test cot has been built, so this page is a measuring sheet. It has no pipe lengths and no cut list.

Where to measure

Not a build guide. There is no cut list. Walk one kennel with a tape, print this sheet, and write the numbers on the paper. Keep the filled sheet at the shelter. Do not post it on a public issue.

Measurement 1 of 6. Interior width.

  1. Interior width

    Set the tape on the kennel floor. Measure from the inside of one wall to the inside of the other. Leave the walls out.

    Interior width

  2. Interior depth

    Look down into the kennel. Measure from the inside of the back wall to the inside of the closed door.

    Interior depth

  3. Interior height

    Measure from the floor to the lowest thing overhead: the ceiling, a shelf, or a resting board. Use whichever is lowest.

    Interior height

  4. Door opening

    Open the door. Measure the opening the cot has to pass through, across and up. A lip or latch that sticks into the opening counts.

    Door opening, across

    Door opening, up

  5. Floor obstacles

    Note anything the legs must clear: a door lip, a drain, a tray rail, or a mat that stays in the kennel. Write what it is and how far it sticks up or in.

    Floor obstacles

  6. Write it down

    Print the sheet and fill the lines at the kennel. Use the same unit on every line. Bring the paper back before anyone cuts pipe. There is still no cut list.
