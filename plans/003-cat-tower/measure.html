Plan 003 Cats · wipeable tower Not a build guide

Cat towerFit the room first

A modular cat tower has to fit one real room, come apart for cleaning, and hold the cats that room already has. Rooms differ, and no test tower has been built, so this page is a fit sheet. It has no pipe lengths, no shelf size, and no weight rating.

What to record

Not a build guide. There is no cut list, no shelf size, and no weight rating. Walk one room with a tape, print this sheet, and write the notes on the paper. Keep the filled sheet at the shelter. Do not post it on a public issue.

Check 1 of 6. Floor spot.

  1. Floor spot

    Stand where the tower will go. Measure the clear floor, across and back. Then write the gap you want between the tower and the walls, kennels, or other gear.

    Measure from the floor to the lowest thing above the spot: a light, a sprinkler, a shelf, a duct, or the ceiling. Write what that thing is.

  3. Path in

    Walk the path from the door the pieces will enter to the floor spot. Measure the narrowest opening, across and up. A lip or latch that sticks into the opening counts. Write whether the tower has to come apart to get there.

  4. How you clean

    Write whether staff hose this room, wipe it, or both. Write the cleaner the shelter already uses. This page does not name one. Note whether the shelves have to come off for that cleaning.

  5. Who uses it

    Ask the shelter how many cats will be on the tower at once, and the heaviest cat they want it to hold. Leave both lines blank until they answer. This page sets no weight. Also write whether the tower must stand free, or whether the shelter already allows it to be fastened to a wall.

  6. Write it down

    Print the sheet and fill the lines in the room. Use the same unit on every line. Write what failed on the last tower, such as carpet, a wobble, or shelves that would not come off. Bring the paper back before anyone cuts pipe. There is still no cut list.
