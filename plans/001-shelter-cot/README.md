# Plan 001: PVC Shelter Cot (medium)

**Status: draft prototype, not yet build-tested.** Lengths are calculated from standard 1" Sch 40 fitting depths (about 0.69" take-off per fitting). Dry-fit and adjust.

Open `guide.html` in a browser for the visual step-by-step guide.

## Specs

- Frame centerline: 36" x 28". Footprint about 37" x 29".
- Bed surface about 5.5" off the floor.
- Estimated parts cost $40 to $55 (not priced yet).

## Cut list

| Piece | Qty | Length | Use |
|-------|-----|--------|-----|
| C | 2 | 26 1/2" | Short rails |
| B | 2 | 24 1/2" | Long rails, middle |
| A | 4 | 3 1/2" | Long rails, corner to tee |

Stick 1 (10 ft): C, C, B, B, A x4. Stick 2 (5 ft): D x4, rest saved.

## Assembly order

1. Cut and label pipe.
2. Leg assemblies x4: tee + D + cap.
3. Long sides x2: A, leg assy, B, leg assy, A. Legs point down.
4. Elbows on all four ends, open sockets facing across.
5. C rails close the frame. Check flat, diagonals equal within 1/4" (about 45 1/2").
6. Prime + cement every joint. Cure 2 h before fabric, 24 h before use.
7. Fabric: 44" x 36", clip 2" corners, 2" W x 2 1/2" D notches at 9" and 35" on both long edges.
8. Flip frame onto fabric, wrap and screw one short side every 4" (7 screws).
9. Stretch tight, screw opposite short side (7).
10. Wrap long sides, notches around legs, screw (7 per side).
11. Flip upright, check for rocking, exposed screws or edges. Date the leg.
